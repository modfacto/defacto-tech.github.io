Fixed-price jobs

These are fixed prices for a selection of the jobs we regularly do, giving you an idea of our charges. If you need help with something that isn’t listed, please get in touch.

These prices include a local visit and the work described. Any equipment, paid apps and subscriptions are extra. We’ll discuss what you need and confirm the price before starting. If you need several things doing together, we can discuss a combined price.

Certain jobs take several hours and are more cost-effective to complete at our workshop. If this is needed, we’ll discuss taking your equipment away and returning it once the work is complete.
